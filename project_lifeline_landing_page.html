Why Project Lifeline

In an emergency, knowing what to do matters.

Limited awareness and practical first-response skills can delay critical intervention. Fear of causing harm and unfamiliarity with lifesaving techniques can make bystanders hesitate.

Preparedness starts before an emergency.

Project Lifeline helps bridge the gap with accessible, practical and evidence-based first-response knowledge, while reinforcing the importance of professional medical assistance.

01

Recognise what is happening

Build awareness of warning signs and situations that need urgent attention.

02

Know how to respond responsibly

Learn practical first-response skills and understand when and how to seek help.

03

Make preparedness a community habit

Bring training, awareness and partnerships into everyday community spaces.

What we teach

Practical knowledge. Real-world readiness.

Our sessions cover relevant first-response and lifesaving skills, adapted to community needs and delivered with qualified trainers where required.

♥

Cardiac emergencies

Compression-Only Life Support (COLS), cardiopulmonary resuscitation (CPR) awareness and automated external defibrillator (AED) awareness.

↗

Choking response

Appropriate choking management techniques and knowing when urgent professional help is needed.

＋

Basic first aid

Awareness and practical learning around bleeding, burns, wounds, fractures and injuries.

⌁

Medical emergencies

Recognising signs of stroke, heart attack, seizures and other critical conditions.

◎

Emergency preparedness

Scene safety, emergency service activation and responsible bystander intervention.
